Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

SpoolKid ("the App") is a personal inventory tool for tracking 3D printing filament spools. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use SpoolKid.

Data We Collect

SpoolKid does not collect any personal information. All spool data you enter (names, weights, materials, colors) is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, synced to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, SpoolKid may sync your spool inventory across your Apple devices using CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is only accessible to you. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to all data stored via iCloud.

Third-Party Services

SpoolKid does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

SpoolKid may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when a spool's remaining filament is running low. These notifications are generated on-device and do not involve any external servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the SpoolKid GitHub repository .
