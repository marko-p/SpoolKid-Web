Features

Everything you need to manage your filament

Designed for makers who are tired of guessing how much filament is left on a spool.

📋

Full Spool Inventory

Keep track of every spool you own. Record the material type, color, brand, diameter, and how much is remaining.

📊

Remaining Weight Tracker

Log how much filament you use after each print. SpoolKid automatically calculates what's left so you always know before you start.

🔔

Low-Stock Alerts

Get notified when a spool drops below your custom threshold. Reorder before you run out, not after.

🎨

Color & Material Tagging

Visually identify spools at a glance with color tags. Filter by PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and any other material you use.

🔍

Quick Search & Filter

Find the right spool in seconds. Search by name, brand, or material, and filter by status or filament type.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your spool data syncs seamlessly across all your Apple devices via iCloud — iPhone, iPad, Mac.

How It Works

Get started in seconds

No complicated setup. SpoolKid is designed to be fast and intuitive.

  1. 1

    Add your spools

    Tap the + button and enter a spool's details: name, brand, material, color, and starting weight. It only takes a few seconds.

  2. 2

    Log each print

    After a print completes, open SpoolKid and record the filament used. The app instantly updates the remaining weight on the selected spool.

  3. 3

    Stay on top of your stock

    Check your inventory at a glance. SpoolKid highlights low spools and notifies you when it's time to restock.
